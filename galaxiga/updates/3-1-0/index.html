THE NEXT CHAPTER

More identity. More action.

01 / SHIP CUSTOMIZATION

Your ship. Your signature.

Make the Hangar your own with cosmetic wing and armor attachments, ship-specific equipment, and Common, Rare and Epic enhancements. Planned acquisition includes in-game coin purchases and boss-exclusive cosmetic rewards. Visual upgrades only; no combat bonuses.

PLANNED FOR 3.1.0

02 / COMBAT IMPROVEMENTS

Read a changing battlefield.

More varied enemy formations, improved grouped dives, stepped clockwork movement and formation morphing are in development. Clearer visual feedback and refined combat pacing aim to keep each encounter readable. Behaviors and tuning are still being tested.

PLANNED FOR 3.1.0

03 / LOOT & REWARDS

Bring something back from battle.

Boss loot containers, cosmetic discoveries and improved reward reveals are planned to give your next encounter a little more anticipation, with more to collect and equip as your Hangar grows.

PLANNED FOR 3.1.0

04 / VISUAL IMPROVEMENTS

A sharper arcade identity.

Reworked enemy artwork, improved player ship presentation, new combat effects and enhanced UI are part of the planned visual refresh. Development previews show work in progress, not the current Google Play release.
